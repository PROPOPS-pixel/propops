📅 Export Period
🏦 MYOB AccountRight — Payroll CSV

MYOB-import-ready CSV. Includes gross wages, PAYG withholding, superannuation (11.5% SGC), net pay, hours worked, and pay period per staff member.
In MYOB: File → Import → Payroll → select this CSV.

Payroll Import format
🏛️ SuperStream — Super Contributions CSV

Electronic super contribution file ready for the ATO Small Business Superannuation Clearing House (SBSCH) or any SuperStream clearing house (Beam, Webjet, etc.).
Includes employee name, TFN status, super fund USI, member number, SGC contribution amount, and pay period. Contributions must be paid within 28 days of quarter end.

Upload to SBSCH or clearing house
⚠️ Super fund details required
Staff must complete onboarding (super fund name, USI, member number) via the Staff Portal for their contributions to appear correctly. Missing details are flagged in the CSV.
🏛️ STP2 — Single Touch Payroll Phase 2
ATO Compliant

STP2 Phase 2 YTD summary for the financial year — all required ATO fields: gross wages, PAYG withholding, super (SGC), YTD totals, income type (SAW), pay categories.
Use this to review with your accountant, or lodge via your payroll software's ATO DSP connection. Includes per-employee YTD breakdown.

🔵 Xero Payroll CSV

Xero-compatible payroll import CSV. Same payroll data mapped to Xero's earnings/deductions format — Employee Name, Payroll Code, Ordinary Time earnings, PAYG withholding, super.
In Xero: Payroll → Pay Runs → Import → select this CSV.

Xero Payroll import format
✅ ATO Compliance Checklist
🏛️ Super Guarantee (SGC) — 11.5% FY2025-26
Pay super within 28 days of each quarter end: 28 Oct, 28 Jan, 28 Apr, 28 Jul. Via SuperStream clearing house.
💸 PAYG Withholding — lodge monthly or quarterly
Small withholders (annual PAYG < $25k) lodge quarterly BAS. Medium withholders lodge monthly. Use the PAYG figures from your STP2 summary.
📋 STP2 — report every pay event
Report each pay run to the ATO on or before pay day via your payroll software's STP2 connection. Use the STP2 summary above with your accountant or MYOB/Xero.
🧾 GST — lodge with BAS
GST collected appears in your Invoice export data. If GST-registered, include in your BAS. Use the Invoices section to download a compliance report.
