FAQ
Straight answers.
Does Hugo actually send the invoices or just create them?
Both. Hugo creates GST-compliant invoices from your job details and sends them to your client's email automatically. You can review before it sends or set it to auto-send on job completion.
Is the payroll tracking ATO-compliant?
Yes. Hugo calculates super at the current ATO rate (11.5%), applies PAYG withholding estimates, and tracks net pay. It's a tracking and planning tool — not a lodgement system. Your accountant handles lodgement; Hugo gives them clean numbers.
What happens if a client doesn't pay after Hugo's reminders?
Hugo sends up to three escalating reminders. After the final notice, you take over — typically with a debt collector or legal letter. Hugo gives you the full audit trail of every chase attempt to support that process.
Can I see what Hugo sends before it goes to clients?
Yes. Every invoice and payment reminder goes through your approval queue first (if you want). You can also set Hugo to auto-send — your call.
How many staff can I add to the rostering?
Unlimited. Hugo.pays doesn't cap staff members. Add your whole crew, set hourly rates, and Hugo handles the scheduling and payroll calculations for all of them.
Is there a contract or lock-in period?
No. Month-to-month only. Cancel any time from your dashboard — no phone calls, no cancellation fees, no bullshit.
