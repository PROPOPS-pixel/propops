Does Hugo actually sound like me, or is it obviously a robot?

+

Hugo learns your voice. You tell it your tone (casual Aussie, professional, etc), your common phrases, your service area slang. Customers generally don't know it's Hugo — they just think you've got your admin sorted.

+

Hugo will say "let me get the boss to call you about that" and flag the lead for your attention. It never makes stuff up. You get notified so you can follow up directly.
